Sleep Hygiene

Bite-sized reminders you can read quickly before bedtime.

Dim the lights

Lower light helps your brain prepare for sleep. Try doing this about one hour before bed.

Park the phone

After the breathing video, put the phone away so the screen does not keep your brain alert.

Keep tomorrow simple

Place school items, water, and uniform where you can find them. Fewer worries make it easier to settle.

Use a steady wake time

A regular morning check-in helps your body learn a more stable sleep rhythm.
